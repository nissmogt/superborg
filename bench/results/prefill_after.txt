Prefill after the interleaved weight layout (engine/kernels.c MAT_X8 kernels, repack at load in engine/model.c),
measured 2026-10-17 on the same machine as bench/results/prefill_baseline.txt: 4-core Intel Xeon (Emerald Rapids,
AVX-512 VNNI), KVM guest, SHARED with other agents' jobs the whole time (load average 2.5-7 during these runs;
the load is printed next to each block). Every number is the minimum of N runs, with N given per block; 1- and
2-thread rows are the clean ones, 4-thread rows are depressed by the other jobs (a 4-thread run gets at most 2-3
cores when two other processes are busy), so compare the 4-thread rows only with the plain-layout rows measured
in the same block.

What changed: q8_0/q4_0/q4_1 matrices are repacked at load into groups of 8 rows interleaved 4 bytes at a time
per block (q8_0 bytes stored as w + 127), and one micro-kernel per (dtype, token count) computes 16 columns x
up to 8 tokens on AVX-512 (8 x 4 on AVX2 / NEON / WASM) with exact int32 dots and the same per-block float
accumulation for every T and every ISA. BORG_NO_REPACK=1 gives the previous layout and kernels ("plain" below).
